Most of the best stuff comes from [Pokeemerald Expansion](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion)'s own configs

Many little things I made based on tutorials from [this list](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/wiki/Tutorials) or [this list](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/wiki/Feature-Branches), in particular (I tried to be as comprehensive and precise as possible): 

- press B while moving to toggle run: [devolov](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/wiki/Push-B-to-Toggle-Running-Shoes)

- namebox shown above message box: [tustin](https://github.com/tustin2121/pokeemerald/tree/feature/namebox)

- quick swap with select button from party menu: [lunos](https://github.com/LOuroboros/pokeemerald/tree/partyScrQuickSwap)

- ORAS-styled dowsing machine field effect: [Bivurnum](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/pull/7211)

- Poke-Vial: [PokemonSanFran](https://github.com/PokemonSanFran/pokeemerald/wiki/pokevial) (with BuffelSaft and Karathan)

- Extended Poke-Nav features: based on the PC access from PokeNav by [devolov](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/wiki/Add-PC-Access-in-PokeNav)

- BW-styled summary screen: [RavePossum](https://github.com/ravepossum/pokeemerald-expansion/wiki#bw_summary_screen_expansion)

- Different kinds of move relearners from the summary screen: based on work from [PCG06](https://github.com/PCG06/pokeemerald/tree/move_relearner)

- expanded bag pockets: [AsparagusEduardo](https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/more-bag-pockets.424360/)
